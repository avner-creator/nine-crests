# Nine Crests: Beat the Dragon!

A 3D sword-and-powers adventure game that runs in the browser.

## How to play

Download the project and double-click `index.html`. No install needed.

## What's in the game

- Nine heroes, each with one elemental power: Fire, Lightning, Water, Ice, Surface Tension, Earth, Wind, Life and Death.
- Nine planets, each with a temple, temple traps and a guardian boss, plus a mini-boss on every planet and the Dragon at the end.
- A Home Base with nine planet portals. Find portal blocks to open the next one.
- Powers are earned in the temples, and they evolve the more you use them. Every hero has a super attack with a cooldown.
- Traders, a blacksmith who can put a crest in your sword, and extra lives hidden in chests.
- Three modes: Adventure, Free Play (fly anywhere) and Glide.
- One or two players on the same keyboard.
- Two camera views: top view and behind-the-hero view.

## Keys

| What | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Move | W A S D | Arrow keys |
| Sword | Space | L |
| Power | F | K |
| Super attack | R | O |
| Dash | Left Shift | ; |
| Switch hero | Tab or 1-9 | I |
| Fly / glide | G | J |
| Talk / trade | E or Enter | Enter |

Change view: V. Turn the view: Z and C. Pause: P. Sound on/off: M.

## Built with

Plain JavaScript and [three.js](https://threejs.org/) (r147, included in `lib/`, MIT licence).
